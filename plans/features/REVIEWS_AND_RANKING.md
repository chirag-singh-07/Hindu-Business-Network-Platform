# Reputation, Reviews, Rankings and Badges
Versioned configurable rules award append-only, deduplicated server-authored points; reversals use compensating ledger entries. Brief's illustrative starting values: completed profile 50, business verification 100, positive review 20, recommendation 30, meaningful participation 5, complete profile information 10. Calibrate before enabling; avoid duplicate/low-value actions and raw likes.

Reviews require eligible authenticated reviewer, no self-review, qualifying interaction policy, uniqueness per interaction where possible, history/edit rules, report/appeal and suspicious-pattern moderation. Do not imply transaction verification without evidence. Ranking considers review quality/authenticity, verified status, recency and meaningful activity with caps and anti-gaming controls; publish understandable methodology. Slices: all-time/monthly and city/state/industry/category with privacy thresholds. Badges (New, Verified, Trusted, Top Rated, Rising Entrepreneur, Premium, Industry Leader, Gold/Platinum) need explicit configurable criteria; premium badge expires with entitlement. Avoid implying accreditation. Audit rule changes and review bias/fairness.
