# UI Prompt — Member Mobile Application
Design a polished, production-minded Hindu Business Network member app for iOS and Android in Flutter/Dart. Use feature-first Flutter widgets and keep views, state/view models and repositories cleanly separated. Brand reference is the provided circular HBN logo: saffron/marigold radial gold, vermilion Hindi and English lettering, orange flags, central trident/damru/Om, handshake, blue accent and three community figures. Convey heritage, trust and business growth respectfully; avoid making every screen look like the ornate badge. Use warm ivory surfaces, charcoal text, saffron primary actions, vermilion only for emphasis/errors, blue for links/secondary trust cues. Pair Inter with Noto Sans Devanagari; preserve readable Hindi. Use the logo full size on onboarding, simplified HBN mark in app bar. Contrast and accessible typography take priority over decorative color.

Create screen-by-screen flows: (1) welcome/value/trust and sign-up/login; (2) verification/recovery; (3) guided profile setup and field-by-field contact visibility; (4) feed with post composer/media, reactions/comments/save/report; (5) directory filters for industry/category/city/state/verified/premium and business profile; (6) connect, request inbox, mutual connections; (7) premium gate and plan comparison/checkout/status; (8) chat list/thread with timestamps, delivery/read, typing, attachment and report/block; (9) reputation, reviews and ranking; (10) ad create/review/payment/status/performance; (11) notification center; (12) settings for privacy, consent, sessions, export/delete.

Use bottom navigation for Home, Discover, Connections, Messages, Profile; keep campaign and settings reachable contextually. Reusable components: brand header, business card, member avatar/badge, privacy-aware contact panel, entitlement gate, post card, filter sheet, status timeline, accessible form field, skeleton/empty/error/retry, upload progress. Clearly distinguish public and premium-only data. Free chat action should explain value and price path without dead-end controls. Premium status and entitlements must reflect server state. Support safe areas, native back, small screens/tablets, dynamic type, screen reader, reduced motion, poor network and offline cached reads. Never imply unimplemented voice chat or fake analytics.
