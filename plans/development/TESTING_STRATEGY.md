# Testing Strategy
Plan unit, integration, API, authorization, webhook, WebSocket, UI/accessibility, device and end-to-end coverage. Negative cases: free cannot message; expired premium cannot send; private contacts stay private; forged payment rejected; rejected ad cannot activate; suspended member blocked; fake reviews/point manipulation rejected; unrelated member cannot read messages. Include Mongo query/ownership isolation, index behavior, races, retries, deletion and privacy requests. Add checks with implementation. A Jest harness is configured, but no behavior tests have been added or run.
